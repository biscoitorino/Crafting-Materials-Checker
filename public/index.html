    Flipping

    Buy listings that sit well under what an item really sells for, relist at market value. Uses the market selected above.

    Resale is the week's median sale price, and never above the cheapest listing you would leave behind, since that becomes your competition. Never more than a week of demand per item. Ignores anything under 8% return.
